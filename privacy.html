Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Brand Blueprint Stories Uploader (the “App”) handles information. The App is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Brand Blueprint Stories (@brandblueprintstories) to upload and manage that channel’s own videos and to read its analytics.

Who uses the App

The App is used solely by the channel owner. It is not offered to the public and does not provide a service to third-party end users.

Data we access

Through Google’s OAuth consent, the App accesses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API only as needed to:

  • Upload, update, schedule, and manage videos on the owner’s own YouTube channel
  • Read analytics and performance metrics for that same channel

The App does not access Gmail, Drive, Contacts, or other Google services beyond the YouTube-related scopes granted by the owner.

How we use data

YouTube channel and video data are used only to operate and improve the owner’s own channel workflows (uploading content and reviewing analytics). We do not sell data. We do not share Google user data with third parties, except as required to operate the App with Google’s APIs or as required by law.

Storage

OAuth tokens and related credentials are stored only on the owner’s private server / private workspace used to run the App. The App stores no third-party user data. We do not create public databases of Google user information.

Google API Services User Data Policy / Limited Use

The App’s use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, Google user data obtained via the YouTube APIs is not used for serving advertisements, is not sold, and is not transferred to others except as necessary to provide or improve user-facing features that are prominent in the App’s user interface, with the user’s consent, or for security / legal compliance.

How to revoke access

You can revoke the App’s access to your Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation, the App can no longer call Google APIs on your behalf until you authorize it again.
